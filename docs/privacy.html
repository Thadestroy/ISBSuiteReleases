Privacy

ISB Suite is a local Windows app. Your stream setup stays on your PC.

What stays on your computer

  • Config, overlays, media, and counters under your Windows AppData folder for ISB Suite.
  • Optional full recovery snapshots and portable zip exports from Settings → Backups (same AppData pack; secrets included unless you turn that off).
  • Twitch tokens and optional Discord webhook URLs / Tiltify credentials you connect in Integrations.
  • Logs used for troubleshooting on your machine.

What leaves your computer

  • Traffic to Twitch (and optional services you enable, such as Discord webhooks or Tiltify) so the app can run your stream features.
  • License activation talks to the store/key check when you enter a Pro key.
  • Update checks hit the public ISBSuiteReleases feed on GitHub.

There is no ISB Suite cloud account for your wheels, points, or overlay layouts.

This website

The docs site is static pages. The download page may call the GitHub API to show the latest installer. Questions or data requests: GitHub Issues.
