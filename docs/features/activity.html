Activity

A Twitch-style feed of events this app saw - follows, subs, bits, raids, redeems, and more - even when no automation or alert is set up.

At a glance

  • Nav rail: Stream Manager, Activity tile on the desk.
  • Filter chips: Twitch, ISB, Integrations, This stream. Show All is the default.
  • Search the list by name, command, reward, or kind. Leaving Stream Manager and coming back from the nav rail clears search and chips.
  • Newest events stay at the top. The list updates live while you stay on the page. Times from earlier days say Yesterday or N days ago instead of only a clock time.
  • Giveaways and chat games stay on one row. The line updates as people join, then shows who won, no entries, or cancelled. Details lists the joiners. Search still finds a joiner when the row is collapsed.
  • Subs, gifts, bits, Power-Ups, and channel-point redeems show the extras Twitch shows: tier, months, cost, and the viewer's attached message on a second line when they wrote one. Viewer Points shop redeems still show the item and price.
  • History is stored on this PC (about the last 2,000 events or 7 days).
  • Tiltify donations, rewards, goals, and milestones appear when Tiltify is connected (Integrations chip).
  • Resend on a row replays that stored alert on the overlay. It does not re-run the automation (no extra counters, chat, or points).
  • ISB chip: wheel winners, giveaways, chat games, community goals (reached, check, and contribute), Viewer Points commands (balance, give, gamble, redeem, admin give - including aliases), point-drop claims, counter tiers, live timer ended, walk-ons, first-time chatters, timed messages (Chat Bot and Automation timers), and automations that ran without an alert.
  • Integrations chip also shows Spotify song requests: !sr queues and !remove (not every skip poll or automation Spotify action).
  • Related: Stream Manager, Channel Points, Alerts, Automations, Integrations.

How to use it

  1. Connect Twitch (and Tiltify, if you use it) on Integrations.
  2. Open Stream Manager from the left nav. Activity is a desk tile.
  3. Use chips to narrow the list, or search to find a person, command, or reward. ISB shows outcomes this app produced. Integrations shows Tiltify and Spotify song requests. This stream keeps only events from the current broadcast.
  4. On a giveaway or chat-game row, click Details to see who joined.
  5. If a row has Resend, click it to play that alert again. The automation does not run a second time.
