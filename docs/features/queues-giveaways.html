Queues and Giveaways

Four independent queues on Home keep spins, sounds, giveaways, and alerts organized. Giveaways are timed viewer-entry rounds that auto-spin a wheel.

At a glance

  • Each queue: Pause, Skip, Clear.
  • Next giveaway join phase opens only after winner chat and TTS finish (about 1 second gap), including empty rounds.
  • Start Giveaway from Automations or Viewer Points shop items.
  • Related: Wheel, Viewer Points.
On this page

The four queues

  • Wheel Queue: pending wheel loads and spins from automations, commands, or shop redemptions. Shows what is next.
  • Sound Queue: audio waiting to play. Sounds linked to a wheel play at spin time instead of the general queue.
  • Giveaway Queue: active and pending giveaways. The current giveaway runs at the top; the next join phase waits for winner chat and TTS (~1 second gap), including when a giveaway expires with no entries.
  • Alert Queue: on-screen alerts waiting for the Alert Box, played one at a time.

Each column has Pause, Skip, and Clear controls.

Giveaway system

Run timed viewer-entry giveaways from automations or viewer point shop items:

  • Chatters enter via a configurable command (default !giveaway).
  • Auto-spins the wheel after a configurable duration (seconds, minutes, or hours).
  • Configurable open / already-joined / winner chat announcements.
  • Duplicate-entry protection with per-user cooldown on the already-joined reply.
  • Optional winner counter increment and winner TTS.

Countdown warnings

Under Advanced settings: editable reminder message plus custom remaining-time offsets (seconds, minutes, or hours). Offsets at or beyond the giveaway length are skipped. Use {timeLeft} and {duration} in reminder text. Same style as chat-game and Ad Break pre-warnings.
