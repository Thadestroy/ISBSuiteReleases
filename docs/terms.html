Terms

These terms cover the ISB Suite app and this website. Installing the app or using the site means you agree to them.

The app

ISB Suite is a local Windows app for your own streams. Your wheels, points, and overlay layouts stay on your PC. There is no ISB Suite cloud account for that setup.

You may install and run ISB Suite for those streams. Free is $0 and includes the full feature set with the limits on Free and Pro. Pro is a one-time $9.99 license. No subscription. A Pro key unlocks those limits on the PC where you activate it. The key is for you. Do not resell the app or share the key.

This website

The docs site is static pages about the app. The download page may call the GitHub API to show the latest installer. What the app stores, and what leaves your PC, is on the privacy page.

Services you connect

When you connect Twitch, YouTube, Discord, Spotify, Tiltify, or a local OBS WebSocket, that service's own terms also apply.

YouTube

ISB Suite uses YouTube API Services. Using ISB Suite's YouTube features means you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google's privacy policy: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy.

Disconnect inside the app deletes the stored YouTube grant immediately. You can also remove ISB Suite at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. A request to delete stored YouTube connection data is completed within 7 days. Disconnect already clears the grant immediately.

Viewer points, watch time, and saved rules stay on your PC. ISB Suite does not sell that YouTube connection data or share it with advertisers.

Your use

Use the app for streams you are allowed to run. Do not use it to break the rules of a service you connect, or to control a channel that is not yours.
